## Group of Bioinformatics, Institute of Molecular Biology NAS RA

Since April 2026 I have led the follicular lymphoma project in the [Group of Bioinformatics]({{ site.data.affiliation.group_url }}) headed by Arsen Arakelyan. The project asks whether transcriptomic data from different platforms and sample types can be combined while preserving the differences between follicular lymphoma, diffuse large B-cell lymphoma and normal germinal-center B cells. Its first output is ComboBatch, a benchmark of 2,234 harmonization approaches on 7,174 samples from 88 cohorts across four transcriptomic platforms.

## Weill Cornell Medicine

I contributed RNA sequencing analysis to a patient-derived T-cell lymphoma biorepository study led by Danilo Fiore and Giorgio Inghirami, which identified pathogenetic mechanisms and host-related therapeutic vulnerabilities in T-cell lymphomas ([*Cell Rep. Med.* 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2025.102029)).

## Open to collaboration

I am interested in collaborations on transposable element evolution and on the harmonization of multi-platform transcriptomic cohorts. I contribute genome-wide co-mapping of TEs with genes and epigenomic tracks on the T2T-CHM13 assembly, integration of ENCODE epigenomic data, and benchmarking of harmonization methods for bulk transcriptomes.

## International Biology Olympiad, 2013

{% include figure.html key="ibo" %}

Gold medal at the 24th International Biology Olympiad (IBO) in Bern, Switzerland, as a member of the Russian national team. The IBO is an annual competition for secondary-school students, with theoretical and practical rounds.

## International Biology Universiade, 2014–2016

Absolute winner of the International Biology Universiade at Lomonosov Moscow State University in each of three consecutive years: 2014, 2015 and 2016.

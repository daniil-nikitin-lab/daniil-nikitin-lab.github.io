## Historical reenactment

{% include figure.html key="reenactment-1" %}

I reconstruct the armour of a late-medieval knight. I made part of my kit myself and bought the rest: a padded gambeson, plate shoulder, elbow and knee defences, a sword and a polearm. I take part in reenactment events in Armenia; my son Ivan has joined me at some of them.
